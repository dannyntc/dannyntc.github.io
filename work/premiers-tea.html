Bringing a product into a new market

Turning market research into practical commercial preparation.

Premier's Tea · Lu Strategy Consulting GroupSingapore market entryJun 2023 – Sep 2024

FocusMarket research and go-to-market preparation

ContributionSegmentation, competitor mapping, sales materials, and prospecting

Output3,000 Singapore B2B prospects

The challenge

Premier's Tea needed practical preparation for the Singapore market. The work required more than a general market overview: the team needed to understand local demand, identify promising customer groups, communicate the product clearly, and create a prospecting base for the sales effort.

The thinking

Market entry becomes actionable when research is translated into choices. Which segments should be prioritised? How are competitors positioned? What information does a prospective B2B customer need before a conversation is useful? How can the research process be repeated when the list of prospects changes?

The goal was to connect the analysis to the work a sales team would actually do: prepare the materials, define the search, and make the resulting opportunities easier to review.

The execution

  • Researched Singapore's tea beverage market, customer demand, competitive positioning, pricing approaches, and emerging trends.
  • Identified and shortlisted potential B2B customer segments for market-entry and sales prioritisation.
  • Mapped competitors and translated research into practical market-entry considerations.
  • Developed local sales materials, including price sheets and brochures, to support B2B prospecting.
  • Built a reusable Make.com workflow that collected prospective businesses across multiple sources.
  • Consolidated the research into a prospecting database of 3,000 local B2B leads.

The output

3,000Singapore B2B prospects collected for the sales effort.
ReusableProspect research workflow that could be run across multiple sources.

The documented result is commercial preparation: market understanding, local sales materials, and a prospecting database. It does not claim sales, revenue, or market share.

The reflection

Research earns its place when it changes what someone can do next. A market map becomes more useful when it helps choose a segment; a customer list becomes more useful when the search can be repeated; and sales collateral becomes more useful when it reflects the questions a local buyer is likely to ask.
