# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want to understand Danny Ng's way of thinking, professional work, writing, and practical experiments. This includes hiring teams, potential collaborators, clients, and readers interested in business, operations, and technology.

## Product Purpose

An independent portfolio that connects Danny's work in strategy, operations, research, process improvement, and practical automation. It should help a visitor understand the kind of problems he works on, how he reasons through them, and what he has built.

## Positioning

The portfolio connects business ideas with execution: understanding what needs to change, figuring out how to improve it, and making the solution work in practice.

## Operating Context

The site is a static GitHub Pages portfolio read on phones, tablets, and desktop browsers. Visitors may arrive from a CV, LinkedIn, GitHub, or a direct link to a case study or essay.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Hand-authored HTML, CSS, and lightweight JavaScript.
- Relative assets and static hosting must remain working.
- Case studies and essays have dedicated reading pages.
- Homepage navigation and older fragment links remain compatible.
- There is no contact-form backend, analytics requirement, or application build step.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Danny Ng.
- Voice: thoughtful, practical, clear, and personally curious.
- Existing portrait and contact destinations remain available.
- The visual direction is a personal journal: warm paper, expressive serif headings, readable sans-serif body text, restrained ink-blue accents, and generous reading space.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current homepage content and assets in this repository.
- Career and education source material supplied privately by the owner (not part of the public repository).
- Three supported work stories: HLS Interiors, Lu Strategy operations, and Premier's Tea market entry.
- Existing essays on AI model selection and independent thinking.
- Existing project links for the Faster-Whisper Command Builder, Video Converter, and Sample Work Generator.

## Product Principles

- Show the reasoning behind the work.
- Connect strategy with implementation.
- Treat tools as means to improve work, not substitutes for judgment.
- Make the site easy to read and easy to explore.
- Separate documented evidence from personal reflection.
